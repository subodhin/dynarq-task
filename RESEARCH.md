# Research

These notes summarise the provided sources and are not legal advice.

1. How does the directive define statutory interest for late payment?

   Statutory interest is simple interest calculated using the applicable reference rate plus at least eight percentage points.
   (Source 1, Article 2(6).)

2. The owner's note says the EU maximum late fee is 25 EUR. Is that right?

   The provided directive specifies a minimum fixed compensation sum of EUR 40 when the applicable conditions are met. The provided source does not establish EUR 25 as a maximum.
   (Source 1, Article 6(1).)

3. Does the business have to send a reminder before it is entitled to interest and to the fixed compensation sum?

   For qualifying business-to-business late payments, the directive says statutory interest is payable without the necessity of a reminder. The fixed compensation sum is also payable without the necessity of a reminder, subject to the applicable conditions.
   (Source 1, Article 3(1) and Article 6(2).)

4. What is the current ECB reference rate?

   Not in the sources. The provided directive explains which reference rate applies during each half of the year but does not provide the current numerical rate. An authoritative, current ECB source would be needed to answer this question.
   (Source 1, Article 3(2).)
